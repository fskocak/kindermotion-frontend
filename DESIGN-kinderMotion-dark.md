# Design System Document

## Theme: Fidelity Dark

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system. It reflects the "Fidelity" brand identity with a modern, clean, and accessible approach, optimized for a dark mode experience.

### Color Palette

Our color palette is built around a vibrant primary blue, complemented by a fresh green and a deep, sophisticated accent, all grounded by a stark neutral. This palette ensures high contrast and visual clarity, especially in a dark theme.

- **Primary Color:** `#0EA5E9` (A bright, energetic blue, ideal for primary actions and branding.)
- **Secondary Color:** `#10B981` (A fresh, verdant green, used for secondary actions and complementary elements.)
- **Tertiary Color:** `#1E293B` (A very dark, almost black blue, providing a deep accent or background for certain components.)
- **Neutral Color:** `#F8FAFC` (An off-white, providing a crisp contrast for text and foreground elements in the dark theme.)

### Typography

The system utilizes `Plus Jakarta Sans` across all typographic elements, providing a consistent, modern, and highly legible visual rhythm.

- **Headline Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Used for all headings and prominent display text.)
- **Body Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Applied to all body copy, ensuring readability for longer texts.)
- **Label Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Utilized for form labels, buttons, and other interactive text elements.)

### Shape and Form

Shapes in the UI feature maximum roundedness, giving elements a soft, friendly, and approachable feel, reminiscent of a pill-shaped aesthetic. This contributes to a distinctive and modern visual language.

- **Roundedness:** `3` (Maximum roundedness, resulting in pill-shaped elements for a soft and approachable aesthetic.)

### Spacing

A normal, balanced approach to spacing is adopted, ensuring good readability and clear separation of elements without feeling overly sparse or cramped.

- **Spacing:** `2` (Normal spacing, providing a balanced layout for content and components.)
